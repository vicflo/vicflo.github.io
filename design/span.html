Drive 1.1

Charge Dynamically

SPAN had just launched a new EV charger featuring level 2 dynamic charging, charging the EV with maximum available power while throttling down on demand to support the home's energy needs. I was responsible for communicating its potential and activating its success within the homeowner app experience with a simple visual.

Users are introduced to a novel function through a dynamic visual interlude.

Behind the scenes

Overview

Problem

Post-launch, 80% of SPAN Drive owners found the 'Charge Dynamically' mode mysterious and needed clarity on its functionality to trust activating it, in addition, prospective owners desired to see this feature's agency.

Process

Within two weeks, I explored how our existing design system skeleton could be adapted or expanded to support motion, and how these visual patterns would be extended to accommodate upcoming Drive features (of which there were five!).

Ultimately the final design was chosen for its simplicity in mirroring the main home screen power flow design and after integration led to such vast adoption that 'charging dynamically' became a default mode.

Product Goals / Outcomes

Visualize functionality at a glance

Homeowners are given a simple explanation of "how it works" complemented with a dynamic story of power balance.

Communicate nuance

Marketing and sales utilized the visual to make dynamic charging tangible and reinforce the value of service upgrade avoidance.

Demonstrate Home + Vehicle power tango

The uniqueness of SPAN's dynamic solution is expressed with romance.

exploration

How might we… visualize energy?

About

The primary challenge in onboarding this new charging feature was formulating a direction that enabled visual trust building. How do consumers interpret electricity and 'smart' system balancing?

Outcome

With a prospective design system revamp on the radar, I used the core elements of our design language, namely colors, forms, and Bauhuas principles, to explore how a single motion graphic could communicate safe load balancing demand between the home and EV charger.

Storyboarding refinement

Balancing dynamic charging

Solution

After extensive visual exploration, we settled on activating a graphic of our 'Home' and 'EV' icons, paired with the SPAN panel and EV charger. In this way, the user would immediately recognize the same visual style of power flow used on our home screen and focus on the idea of load balancing, such as when the home needs more energy, the EV charger can take less, and vice versa.

Result

With onboarding, adoption of the 'Charge Dynamically' mode jumped from 9% to ~80% and became the default for all homeowners.

Charging dynamically made clear through a power flow tango.
